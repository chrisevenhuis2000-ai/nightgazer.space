## Direction contract

THESIS: A photographic plate archive, not a news feed. Refuses the category's dark-space hero plus card grid; 839 plates are a ruled ledger, not 839 cards.

OWN-WORLD: NightGazer night-blue as emulsion ground (#0E1022 / #15182F), hairline rules as the only elevation, zero radius with corner registration marks and an edge fiducial ruler. #378ADD is chinagraph blue, reserved by law for live perishable values and nothing else; #185FA5 is structural; #B5D4F4 stamps provenance. Archivo throughout, JetBrains Mono for measurement only. Two semantic colours total (good/warn) for conditions and quiz truth. State is a mark, never a coloured badge: reading level is a three-step ladder.

STORY: The visitor reads tonight's verdict, cloud cover, ISS and the next event before anything else; understands NightGazer as an archive with live instruments; then reads today's plate or filters the ledger.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Staging banner, then a 68px plate header (logo, printed nav, provenance). Below it one bordered plate: today's APOD as emulsion ground at 30% under a headline block, an edge fiducial ruler on top, and a five-cell live instrument bar across the full width (vannacht /10, bewolking %, ISS lat-lon with continuous readout, volgende in dagen, archief count). Primary action is the archive anchor in cell five; the lead plate's read link sits one screen down.

FORM: Glass-plate archive, candidate 5 of seven grounded worlds (Sterrengids-ephemeris, Tirion-atlas, planisfeer, Westerbork-striprol, glasplaat-archief, KNMI-kaart, Blaeu-globe). Assigned by seed key a1c022e5, scope direction, mode operate. Raises taken from declined challengers: reserved-palette law (arcade-CRT), state-as-mark (cutting bench), printed label on every control plus continuous readout for live feeds (cassette fascia), deep ground voids instead of dividers (cloud quarry). Materials translated to the user-pinned NightGazer palette rather than the archive's silver-gelatin greys; the pin was given mid-run and beats the roll.
